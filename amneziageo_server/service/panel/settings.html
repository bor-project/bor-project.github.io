Settings

The “Settings” section is seen by a role with the “Manage access” permission. It gathers what belongs to the panel itself and to the access into it.

  • Server Address, port, path and language of the panel, configuration name template, pre-releases.
  • Certificates The certificate of the panel: a Let's Encrypt domain or your own files.
  • Subscriptions Client configurations by link: port, path, certificate, update interval.
  • Users and roles Accounts, roles with permissions and tokens for scripts.
  • Diagnostics Component versions, the event log and the connection log.

The “Server” and “Certificates” tabs write their settings with “Save”, and they take effect after “Restart”, the button in the header that appears while saved settings wait; the language, the config name template and pre-releases take effect at once. “Subscriptions” take effect without a restart. “Cancel” drops what is not saved, and an unsaved edit stays while other pages are opened.

From the console the same is done by items 10 to 14 of the menu and the panel, subscriptions, user, role and token commands of the utility.
