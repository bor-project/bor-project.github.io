Routing presets Only in the AmneziaGeo appOnly the AmneziaGeo app gets the presets, through the template of the client.Other AmneziaWG clients do not get this: they get only the configuration file.Learn more

A routing preset is a routing list of the AmneziaGeo client that is kept on the panel and handed to the app through the hello of the interface. The template of a client names the presets its clients get; routing by them happens on the device, not on the server. The “Routing presets” tab of the “Clients” page lists the presets with the time of the change and the number of templates that hand them out; the number leads to the list of templates with the preset picked.

Main

“Name”: up to 64 characters, one of a kind in the panel; this is what the list is called on the client. “On by default”: the client puts the list in use when it adds it, unless another list is in use already. “Read only”: the client keeps the list from being renamed, changed or removed and takes the newer versions of the preset itself, without “Update”; the list can still be turned on and off on the device.

Templates

The “Templates” part names the templates that hand the preset out, a name leads to the settings of the template. The field adds a template: the offer names its present presets in grey beside it, and the preset comes last in the template. The cross takes the preset off the template. The changes are kept with the preset by “Save”; a copy of a preset takes no templates.

Lists

Part What it does
Through the VPN the entries go through the tunnel
Directly the entries go past the tunnel
Block the client drops the entries
Everything through the VPN everything goes through the tunnel but what goes directly
All UDP through the tunnel every UDP packet goes through the tunnel, whatever the lists say: for calls and games that learn their addresses not through DNS

The entries are written the same way as in a template: geoip:ru, geosite:youtube, cidr:10.0.0.0/8, domain:example.com, up to 256 in each part. “Through the VPN” and “Directly” also take programs: app:<name>, for example app:Telegram: the app of the client finds the program on the device itself (on Windows by the installed programs, the shortcuts of the Start menu and App Paths, on Linux by the .desktop entries and PATH, on Android by the label and the package) and leaves the line out when it finds none; the older forms with a kind, app:path=<path>, app:dir=<folder>, app:name=<file>, app:svc=<service>, app:pkg=<package>, as well. The field shows such entries as “Program”. The line of a program stays one entry, spaces and all; a comma parts it only where another entry follows: app:Telegram, app:Zoom is two programs, app:Telegram, geoip:ru a program and a geo key. A program may follow another entry too: geoip:us, app:Discord Canary is a geo key and a program. Hello hands the programs out as they are; they do not go into AllowedIPs.

All apps through the VPN

A preset with the “Everything through the VPN” flag carries the “All apps through the VPN” label under its name in the list of presets, a preset with “All UDP through the tunnel” carries “All UDP through the VPN”; the label leads to the form. In the form a note above the flags explains this with a “More” link here. The client sends every application of the device through the server but the entries of “Direct”, or the UDP of every application even when its addresses are not in the lists. Applications that do not need the VPN go through the server as well: some applications may notice the VPN, learn the server address or stop working. Add such applications to “Direct”. The AmneziaGeo client shows the same label at such a list, see All apps through the VPN on the site of the client.

“All UDP through the tunnel” is off for a new preset.

How the app takes a preset

A preset has a permanent identifier, given when it is added, and the time of the last change of the list: of the name, the entries and the switches. Hello hands them out together with the list, and the client knows the list by its identifier:

  • a client without such a list adds it, and puts it in use if the preset is on by default and the client routes by no list;
  • a client with the list keeps its own; when the preset is newer, the card of the list on the device offers an update, and the owner of the device, accepting it, gets the name, entries and switches of the preset instead of what the list held, while the list stays in use or not, as it was;
  • the list of a “Read only” preset takes a newer version itself as soon as the client gets it, and gives way to no change on the device; the flag taken off, the list is an ordinary one again and a newer version waits for “Update”; the flag put back, a list changed on the device takes the preset; the preset taken off the template, the list stays with the client as an ordinary one;
  • a list the owner removed comes back at the next hello;
  • a list added under the name of the preset by an app older than the identifiers gets tied to the preset.

The card of the list on the device shows where it comes from: the name of the configuration of the client, the one the subscription gives. Together with the presets, hello hands out the geo sources of the panel: the client adds the sources it lacks by address and moves a source with the same name and kind to the address of the panel, so that the keys of the preset name lists the client has, see Geo lists. The whole scenario: Routing on the client device.

Removing

A preset a template names is not removed: “A template hands the preset out”; the removal page names these templates, first they are taken off in the “Templates” part. Clients that already got the list keep it.
