Users and roles

The “Users and roles” tab carries three tables: “Users”, “Roles” and “Tokens”. People sign in to the panel with an account of the panel and a password; scripts with a long-lived token that acts on behalf of a role.

Users

Column What it shows
Name the name for signing in
Display name how the account is signed
Account type “Panel”, or “System”: an account that also stands behind a user of the host
Role the role or “no role”, a link to its settings
Status “enabled” or “disabled”

“Add” opens a new account: the name (lower case letters, digits, dot, dash and underscore, starting with a letter), the display name, the role, the password with the “Generate” button, “Require a password change” (the new user changes the issued password at the first sign-in). The page of an account changes the role and the state, “Password” in the row menu sets a new password, “Remove” removes the account with its sessions. Everyone changes their own password in the account menu in the header. A password shorter than eight characters is not accepted (AMNEZIAGEO_MIN_PASSWORD in the environment sets another threshold); ten misses lock the account for fifteen minutes. The last enabled account with the “Manage access” permission cannot be demoted, disabled or removed, and none of this can be done to your own account.

A name a user of the host carries is not given to an account of the panel: “The host carries a user with this name, that name is left to it”.

In the container the accounts of the host are off (Auth__HostLogin=Off, Auth__HostUsers=false): the panel signs in only with its own accounts and makes no system users, and the form has no “User of the system” box.

Roles

A permission is what a role opens; a role carries a set of permissions, and an account carries a role.

Permission What it opens
Read the state reading everything: “Overview”, the lists, the route test
Manage clients clients, templates, routing presets
Manage interfaces interfaces and their ports
Manage routing rules, basic lists, channels, geo, DNS
Manage access “Settings”: server, certificates, subscriptions, users, roles, tokens, diagnostics, restoring from a backup
Update the panel “Update” on “Overview”
Client updates “Settings” - “Client updates”: the serving and the releases of the client; together with “Manage access”, which opens “Settings”
Download backups “Download a copy” on “Overview”

The “Roles” table shows the code, the name, the rights, the number of accounts and of tokens. The built-in admin role carries all the permissions and is neither removed nor cut down: at every start its permissions are lined up with those of the release, so a new permission reaches the administrator by itself. Roles of your own are made with “Add”: a code (like the name of an account), a name and the checked permissions. The settings of a role name its accounts and tokens. The field “Give the role to an account” gives the role to an account in place of its old one, the offer names its present role in grey; there is no cross: an account has one role, another one is given in another role or in the settings of the account. The bans are those of the settings of an account: you do not take the rights off yourself, the last administrator stays. A role given to accounts (“The role is given to accounts”) or carrying tokens (“Tokens act by this role”) is not removed; the removal page names them.

Tokens

A token for scripts and other services is issued under a role and acts with its permissions at the moment of the call, so a change to the permissions of the role reaches the tokens at once. A token does not belong to an account: disabling and removing accounts does not touch it, and it can be stopped only by revoking it or by its lifetime. It starts with agt_, goes in the same Authorization: Bearer header as a session token, and is kept only as a hash: the secret is shown once, when it is issued (“The token is shown once”).

Column What it shows
Name up to 64 characters, one of a kind in the panel
Role the role on behalf of which the token acts, a link to its settings
Created, Valid until the lifetime: 1 to 3650 days or “no end”
Last used when and from which address the token was last used, “never”
Status “active” or “expired”

“Add” asks for the name, the role and the lifetime in days, and “Revoke” in the row menu revokes it. From the console the same is done by amneziageo-server token list, token add <name> --role <role> [--days <n>] and token revoke <id>. How a token goes into the API: HTTP API.
