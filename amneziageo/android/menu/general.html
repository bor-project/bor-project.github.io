General

Appearance

“Theme” - “System”, “Light” or “Dark”. “Interface language” - “System”, “Русский” or “English”; the language of the console is set apart, with the --lang key or the AMNEZIAGEO_LANG variable.

Background activity

The general settings carry a “Background activity” block: notifications, the battery saver and, on MIUI and HyperOS, autostart, each with a “Configure” button that opens its system screen. Only the notifications row names a state: the application does not ask the system about the battery saver and autostart, they are checked on the system screen. On a phone the notifications row carries the “Tunnel notification” choice: “Detailed” or “Brief”, see The tunnel notification. doctor reports the same three.

About

“App version”, “Amnezia engine version” - the version of AmneziaWG that comes with the app, “Build type” - the variant of the build when one is set, and “Project repository” - a link to GitHub.

“License” - GNU GPL-3.0 or later. The links “Terms of use”, “Privacy” and “Third-party components” open these texts in the window; under the heading of the terms and of the privacy stand their version and the day they were accepted. On its first start the window shows the terms of use and the privacy and waits for “Accept”; “Quit” closes the window and leaves the tunnel alone. A new version of the texts shows once again. The texts on the site: terms of use, privacy, third-party components.

Update

“Check for updates” asks the release server; a version found is written beside it, “Download” fetches it, “Install” starts the installation, “Cancel” stops the download. “Check for prerelease versions” adds beta builds to the check. The check also runs by itself: a new version is announced by the “Update available” bar at the top of the window and by the notification “Version <version> is available”. The version notification comes once, a tap opens the application, and it stays until that version is installed; it is the only notification that puts a mark on the icon of the application. It is turned off in the notification settings of the application, the “Update available” category. The downloaded file is checked against the published checksum. Installing an APK needs the permission to install apps from this source: the system asks for it, and after the answer “Install” has to be pressed again.

How the app updates itself: Update in the installation.

Import / export

Moving configurations and routing lists to another device in one file. “Export…” opens the “Export configurations and routing lists” window: tick the configurations and the lists, and in a list the separate rules worth moving (rules for the applications of this computer are of no use on another one), then “Copy content”, “Save to file…” or “Send…”. The file carries the keys of the configurations. “Import…” takes such a file: “Choose file…” or a paste from the clipboard, then “On name conflict” - “Add as new”, “Replace”, “Skip” or “Merge” - and “Save”. A file made by a newer version of the application is not read: update the application. From the console: amneziageo bundle export --all --out <file> and amneziageo bundle import --file <file> [--policy new|replace|skip|merge].
