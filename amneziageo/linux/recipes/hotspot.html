Sharing the VPN over Wi-Fi

  1. In the “Connections” section open the “Wi-Fi” tab.
  2. Set the “Network name” and the “Password”, and if you like the “Band” and “Hidden network”.
  3. Turn “Access point” on and save.
  4. Join a phone, a TV or a console to this network as to any Wi-Fi.

The joined devices reach the internet through this computer: with the tunnel up by its rules, so the same goes into the tunnel as for the computer itself and the rest directly; without the tunnel, directly. “Devices” shows the ones joined.

Access point settings: Connections. From the console: amneziageo hotspot on --ssid <name> --password <password>.
