AmneziaGeo

The AmneziaGeo client brings up AmneziaWG tunnels on Windows, Linux and Android and decides by rules what goes through them. AmneziaGeo Server runs your own AmneziaWG server: it hands the clients their configurations and sends their traffic on by the same rules.

  • AmneziaGeo VPN client for Windows, Linux and Android on the AmneziaWG engine. Routes by geo databases, domains, address ranges and applications; multiple simultaneous VPN connections on Windows. Windows · Linux · Android
  • AmneziaGeo Server Panel for your own AmneziaWG server on Debian and Ubuntu - clients, templates and subscriptions, routing by geo databases and domains through other servers, a resolver for the clients, updates and backups. Service · Docker
